# ISO/IEC 27001 - Knowledge Requirements

> TL;DR: This file lists the concrete knowledge and evidence expected before claiming readiness for this certification.

**Owner MIP** : Victor
**Validation type** : Certification organisme

## Required Knowledge Blocks

| Block | Knowledge to master | Expected proof |
|-------|---------------------|----------------|
| M1 | ISMS clauses 4 to 10 | Define scope, policy, objectives and governance |
| M2 | Risk assessment, treatment and statement of applicability | Map risks to selected controls |
| M3 | Annex A controls implementation and evidence | Demonstrate control design and operation |
| M4 | Internal audits, management review, continual improvement | Close non conformities with CAPA |

## Pre-certification Validation

- [ ] Maintain risk register and treatment plan
- [ ] Keep SoA with rationale per control
- [ ] Prepare audit evidence package by control

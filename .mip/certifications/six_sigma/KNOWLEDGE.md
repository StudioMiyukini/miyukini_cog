## Required Knowledge Blocks

| Block | Knowledge to master | Expected proof |
|-------|---------------------|----------------|
| M1 | DMAIC and CTQ framing | Translate customer need into measurable CTQ |
| M2 | Measurement and process capability | Build baseline with reliable data collection |
| M3 | Root cause analysis and statistical validation | Use hypothesis driven analysis |
| M4 | Control plans and SPC | Stabilize gains and prevent regression |

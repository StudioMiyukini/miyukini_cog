## Required Knowledge Blocks

| Block | Knowledge to master | Expected proof |
|-------|---------------------|----------------|
| M1 | Audit principles: integrity, impartiality, evidence | Apply ethics and evidence discipline |

| M4 | Findings follow up and competence management | Track corrective actions to closure |
